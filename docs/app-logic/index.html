Design principles · decided 2026-10-14

  1. The app optimises for still training a year from now, not for the weight on the bar.
  2. Undertraining costs a little growth; overtraining costs weeks. Bias toward too easy, ramp slowly.
  3. The user makes one decision per visit: start or not. Everything else has a default.
  4. Two questions describe a day: how much energy, and how much time. Nothing else is asked.
  5. The app may refuse "more". It never refuses "less".
  6. Progress is earned on a ladder: hit the top of the rep range and the next rung is yours. Step up and step down counts are settings from 1 to 5.
  7. Every exercise has a cost from 1 to 5. Energy sets the average cost of the session and how hard sets are pushed. Time sets how many exercises and sets fit.
  8. Energy and time never move a rung. The ladder never changes what is in a session.
  9. Physical and mental signals are kept apart: a rep count below the top is the only ladder signal; stopping early asks energy, time, or pain.
  10. Nothing is timed for the user. Seconds per set are learned from the set taps and price the time dial.
  11. Unused rungs decay gently and re-climb in one clean session. After a week away the day defaults to easy. There is no comeback mode.
  12. Scheduling is sequence-based: the next session is the next one in the rotation whenever you show up. There is no Tuesday session to miss.
  13. Cardio and mobility are the floor: deliberately dumb (a duration, a fixed routine) and one tap away.
  14. Plans think in movement patterns; progress is tracked per exercise; location picks the exercise and how high a bodyweight ladder goes.
  15. Automations only change defaults and send at most one nudge. They never act for the user.
  16. Six settings in plain words, plus a cost override per exercise. The guardrails are not settings.
  17. Progress is shown as sessions per month and rung trend per pattern. Never streaks.

Session loop · one visit to the app

---
title: Session loop · one visit to the app
---
flowchart TD
  A([Open app]) --> B["Detect context<br/>location · days since last session · calendar"]
  B --> C["Two questions, both defaulted<br/>How much energy? easy / normal / hard<br/>How much time? short / normal / long"]
  C --> D{"Away 7+ days?"}
  D -- yes --> E["Energy defaults to easy<br/>decayed rungs already applied"]
  D -- no --> F["Defaults from last time<br/>and the calendar"]
  E --> G
  F --> G["Build the session<br/>time → slots · energy → rungs and variants by cost"]
  G --> H["Show session<br/>each exercise: rung · sets · aim for the top of the range"]
  H --> I{"User adjusts?"}
  I -- "energy or time" --> G
  I -- "swap" --> K["Movement session<br/>cardio duration or mobility routine"]
  I -- "no" --> L
  K --> L["Do session"]
  L --> M["Per set, one tap<br/>hit the top, or how many you got<br/>timestamps recorded, no timer shown"]
  M --> S{"Stop early?"}
  S -- "one tap" --> T["Reason: energy / time / pain<br/>remaining sets = not attempted"]
  S -- no --> N
  T --> N(["Finish"])
  N --> O{"Feel rating?"}
  O -- "one tap" --> P["Write history<br/>energy · time · who chose them · reps · timestamps"]
  O -- "skip" --> P
  P --> Q["Ladder judges each exercise<br/>timing model updates seconds per set"]
  Q --> R["Rotation advances · one line says what changed"]

title: The ladder · one rule per exercise
---
flowchart TB
  subgraph RUNGS["Every exercise is a ladder of rungs"]
    direction LR
    R1["Barbell, machine<br/>next weight step"] ~~~ R2["Dumbbells<br/>next pair"] ~~~ R3["Bodyweight<br/>next variant: wall → incline → knee → floor → feet up → pike → archer"] ~~~ R4["Top rung with no load available<br/>ceiling: beat your best total reps"]
  end
  subgraph TARGET["The target always has the same shape"]
    direction LR
    T1["This rung"] ~~~ T2["This many sets"] ~~~ T3["Aim for the top of the rep range<br/>strength 4 to 6 · muscle 8 to 12 · fitness 12 to 20"]
  end
  subgraph JUDGE["After each normal or hard session"]
    direction TB
    J0{"Every set hit the top?"}
    J0 -- "yes, N sessions in a row<br/>(step up: 1 to 5, default 1)" --> J1["Step up one rung<br/>reps fall back toward the bottom"]
    J0 -- "no" --> J2{"Any set below the bottom?"}
    J2 -- "yes, M sessions in a row<br/>(step down: 1 to 5, default 2)" --> J3["Step down one rung"]
    J2 -- "no" --> J4["Stay on the rung<br/>keep building reps"]
  end
  subgraph NEVER["Never judged"]
    direction LR
    N1["Easy sessions"] ~~~ N2["Sessions stopped for energy or time"] ~~~ N3["Sets never attempted"]
  end
  RUNGS ~~~ TARGET ~~~ JUDGE ~~~ NEVER

Cost drift, decay and timing · what the app learns over time

---
title: Cost drift, decay and timing · what the app learns over time
---
flowchart TB
  subgraph DRIFT["Cost in play = default cost, moved by you, clamped 1 to 5"]
    direction LR
    D0["Default cost<br/>from the exercise library<br/>override any time in the catalog"] --> D1["Not done for 3+ weeks<br/>cost in play +1<br/>(weeks before decay: 2 to 8, or off)"]
    D0 --> D2["Bodyweight ceiling cleared<br/>a few sessions in a row<br/>cost in play −1 · the next variant becomes the hard thing"]
    D0 --> D3["Loaded exercise<br/>cost stays, the weight climbs"]
  end
  subgraph DECAY["Rung decay replaces comeback"]
    direction LR
    K1["Every 3 weeks an exercise is not done<br/>its rung drops by one<br/>never below where it started"] --> K2["First clean session at the top<br/>steps straight back up<br/>regardless of the step-up setting"]
    K3["Away 7+ days<br/>next session defaults to easy<br/>cost-5 lifts start one rung down"]
  end
  subgraph TIMING["Timing model: seconds per set, per exercise"]
    direction LR
    M1["Seeded by cost<br/>cost 5 ≈ 4 min · cost 3 ≈ 2.5 min · cost 1 ≈ 1.5 min<br/>set plus rest"] --> M2["Learned from taps<br/>gap between one set tap and the next<br/>weighted toward recent sessions"] --> M3["Used by the time dial<br/>3 sets of deadlift ≈ 12 min until your history says otherwise"]
    M4["Rest is never prompted<br/>shown only as history on the exercise card"]
  end
  subgraph CARD["The exercise card shows"]
    direction LR
    C1["Rung<br/>variant or weight"] ~~~ C2["Average time per set<br/>at your current reps"] ~~~ C3["Best total reps<br/>at this rung"] ~~~ C4["Cost<br/>default and override"]
  end
  DRIFT ~~~ DECAY ~~~ TIMING ~~~ CARD

Person state · drives defaults and reminder tone

title: Person state · drives defaults and reminder tone
---
stateDiagram-v2
  state "At risk" as AtRisk
  [*] --> Onboarding
  Onboarding --> Active: first session logged
  Active --> AtRisk: 3 days without a session
  AtRisk --> Active: session logged
  AtRisk --> Away: 7 days without a session
  Away --> Active: any session logged
  note right of Active
    defaults from last time
    ladder judges normal and hard sessions
  end note
  note right of AtRisk
    one gentle nudge
    suggests easy or a movement session
  end note
  note left of Away
    no guilt, reminders go quiet
    next session defaults to easy
    decayed rungs already applied
    one clean session re-climbs a decayed rung
  end note
