# Unbiased pass: GPT-6-Astra (high), 2026-10-20

Astra saw only `exercises.json` (the corpus with a neutral legend, no rules, no review history) and filed 139 findings on 119 exercises. Accepted 46, rejected 93. Result: `align/corpus6.json`.

## What it found that the two-reviewer pass missed

- Five `each` flags wrong under our own rule (single bell front squat and suitcase carry are per hand; two-hand cable twists, chops and lifts are not).
- The 'doubled band' ambiguity GPT fixed on two ladders was on ten more; all now say keep the heavy band and add a second.
- 2 kg steps on 4 kg dumbbell raises, flyes and kickbacks were coarser than the corpus's other light dumbbell work.
- 'Deep dips' as a top rung; rotational slams as a 'harder' rung; a stretch tagged for rear shoulders it barely touches.
- Safety cues the texts lacked: rack safeties on barbell presses and squats, Smith stops, the handstand exit, non-rolling weights under a renegade row, a table that cannot tip under an inverted row.

## Where it disagrees with settled calls (not applied)

- Kettlebell swing and the push presses as power; wall sit as strength. The owner set these.
- A finer equipment vocabulary (dual cable stations, seated vs standing calf machines, incline/decline press machines, a slam ball, a Nordic anchor, a light bar). Ours is deliberately coarse for everyday gyms.
- Mixed bar-or-dumbbell loads (shrug, upright row, skull crusher, preacher curl, trap-bar farmer's carry): now flagged by three models. Still an app display fix rather than dropping the dumbbell options.
- A hip-flexor muscle: the body map has no region for it.

## App follow-ups it adds

- The machine-assisted pull-up's load runs backwards (less assistance is harder); the progression must subtract. This is pre-existing in the app.
- Smith and landmine loads are plates added; show them as '+kg' so 0 reads as the empty bar.
