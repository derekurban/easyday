## Where everyone else sits

The rating ranks against a mix: three parts people who train, one part everyone. Everyone is centred 2 spreads of the lifters'
middle tiers under their median, a little under their 5 % mark: about 0.9 of it on the barbell lifts, where studies
of untrained adults put them. The same place on every lift, unless a population study says otherwise:

- `pushup`: 1.45x the Beginner mark (-0.557 spreads). the Canadian push-up norms (CSEP, Payne 2000) put the median man aged 20 to 39 near 22 push-ups, well above the push-up loggers' Beginner mark: people who log push-ups are mostly new to them
